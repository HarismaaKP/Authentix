One ledger, two very different jobs.

Manufacturers commit a product's identity to the chain once. Customers check any unit against it in seconds.

Manufacturer

  • 1
    Register the productName, description, and the original reference photo.
  • 2
    Get a product IDA unique identifier tied to the image's fingerprint.
  • 3
    Commit it to the ledgerThe ID and image hash are written to the blockchain.
  • 4
    Print and attach the QRGenerated automatically, ready to affix to the product.

Customer

  • 1
    Scan the QR codeCamera scan, or upload a photo of the code.
  • 2
    Pull the registered recordVerified against the blockchain in real time.
  • 3
    Photograph the item in handThe AI locates the regions that matter most.
  • 4
    Get a verdictA similarity score against the original, with a clear result.

Three possible verdicts.

Authentic The captured image's key regions closely match the manufacturer's original. High confidence.
Suspicious Partial match. Some regions align, others don't — worth a closer look before trusting it.
Counterfeit The important visual regions diverge significantly from the registered original.
